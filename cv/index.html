Research Appointments

Research Assistant, Sustainability, Optimization, Learning, and Algorithms Research Lab (SOLAR), UMass Amherst 2022–current

Large-scale data science and algorithm research for sustainability and equity. Publication collaborations with CMU, MIT, and Columbia.

  • Wrote the SunSight library for policy simulation and visualization of national-scale, ZIP-code granularity solar deployment and its effects on energy generation, carbon emissions, and equity.
  • Cleaned and analyzed large-scale data from Google's Project Sunroof, the American Community Survey, EIA, and others.
  • Developed a multi-objective optimizing evolutionary neural network method for rooftop solar panel placement.
  • Simulated effects of incentives and tariffs on consumer behavior and solar deployment; drafting alternative policies from statistics, simulation, and machine learning.
  • Created, formalized, and analyzed the Green Bin Packing problem for carbon-efficient job allocation in data centers.
Research Assistant, Biologically Inspired Neuro-Dynamical Systems Lab (BINDS), UMass Amherst 2018–2022

Low-energy, biologically plausible machine learning models and theoretical analysis.

  • Major contributor of the BindsNET library for spiking neural network simulation.
  • Developed a minibatching procedure for spiking-neuron training on large-scale networks.
  • Theoretically analyzed the computational power of a spiking neuron compared to artificial neurons.
  • Developed a circuit simulation method using graph neural networks with 100% accuracy.
