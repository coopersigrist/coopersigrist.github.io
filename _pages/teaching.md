I have been a sole instructor and course designer at UMass Amherst since 2021, usually with a team of PhD teaching assistants. Course evaluations were at or above department and campus averages on every measured criterion, and the work led to Distinguished Teaching Award nominations in 2023 and 2024.

A collected set of olympiad practice materials, lecture videos, and course notes lives on the [Student Resources]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Instructor

### Introduction to Machine Learning (CS389)
Third- and fourth-year undergraduate course, about 80 students. Co-designed the original curriculum with Philip Thomas in Spring 2021, then independently designed and lectured in Spring 2022, 2023, and 2024.

- [Intro to Machine Learning lecture recordings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1buAGr8D2Q)
- [Machine learning slides](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/10BYJ8m8F-FIml6q7nbCDns4WWUeFg9B_?usp=sharing)

### Algorithms (CS311)
Core required third- and fourth-year course, about 150 students. Independent instructor in Spring 2026, with the course redesigned for an AI-era classroom.

- [Lecture recordings on Cooptoon](https://www.youtube.com/@Cooptoon)

### Foundations of Programming (CS110)
First- and second-year course, about 60 students, mixing CS and non-CS backgrounds. Taught an experimental section in Fall 2024; the structure and final project are now used in the intro sequence.

### Thinking with Machine Learning
First-year seminar, about 25 students. Designed in Fall 2021 and still offered at UMass Amherst.
